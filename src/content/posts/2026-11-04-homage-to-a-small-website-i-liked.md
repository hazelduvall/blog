I liked looking at the website <https://syntacticsugarglider.com> from time to
time. I stumbled upon it somehow in 2020 or 2021; I forget exactly how or what
I was doing anywhere near it, but nonetheless it came into my life and it
brought me joy.

Like most small personal websites however, this one eventually let its domain
name lapse and fell off the internet. Luckily the source code is still on
github so I can make a mirror at
[/syntacticsugarglider/](/syntacticsugarglider/) on my own webesite.

Maybe one day this will fall into disrepair too, who knows. I don't plan on
going away anytime soon tho.

To the original author of syntacticsugarglider: I don't know you, and you don't
know me, but from one stranger to another, thank you for making something
awesome.
